# 02. Agile Methodology

## 📚 Introduction

Agile introduced an iterative and incremental approach to software development.

Instead of trying to build the complete application in one long cycle, the application is divided into smaller features and developed in iterations.

---

## 🔄 Traditional Approach

Waterfall:

Requirements
↓
Design
↓
Development
↓
Testing
↓
Deployment

The complete application may take a long time before the customer receives working functionality.

---

# 🚀 Agile Approach

Agile works in smaller iterations.

```text
Requirement
     ↓
Sprint Planning
     ↓
Development
     ↓
Testing
     ↓
Sprint Review
     ↓
Customer Feedback
     ↓
Next Sprint
